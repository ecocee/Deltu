## What this means

The software license (Apache-2.0) covers the *code*. Trademark law
covers the *name and brand*, and it remains with ECOCEE regardless of
how the code is used or forked.

## Permitted without asking

- Stating that your project **uses** Deltu, or **is built on** Deltu

- Referring to Deltu factually in documentation, articles, courses, or
  comparisons.
- Reproducing the DELTU name in the ways Apache-2.0 §6 already allows:
  identifying the origin of the Work and reproducing the `NOTICE` file.

- Naming your product, fork, distribution, or company "Deltu" or using

- Using the logo or brand marks on packaging, marketing, or websites in
  a way that implies the project is official or certified.
- Selling or distributing a modified version under the DELTU name.
- Registering domains, social handles, or trademarks incorporating
  DELTU.

## Forks and modified distributions

You may fork and redistribute the code freely under Apache-2.0, but you
must **rename** the fork so users can tell provenance: do not use the
DELTU name or logo for a fork's binary, package, or distribution. Keep
the original attribution and `NOTICE` content per the license.
